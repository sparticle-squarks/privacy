# Privacy Policy

This is a personal-use application operated by its owner for the owner's
own Gmail account. It reads, searches and sends email on the owner's
behalf through Google's Gmail API, and for no other purpose.

- No data is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party.
- Email data is processed only to answer the owner's own requests and is
  not used for advertising or to train any model.
- Access can be revoked at any time at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
